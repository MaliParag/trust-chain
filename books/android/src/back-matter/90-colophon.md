# Colophon

*The Android Trust Chain* (Volume IV of *The Platform Trust Chain Series*) is set in the *Mineral Stone and Silicon Jade Schematic* interior theme (`#f7f9f8` mineral-stone light paper, `#0a1210` obsidian dark canvas, and `#146c4e` / `#57c98c` silicon-jade structural accents), pairing `Charter` and `Source Serif 4` body typography with `Inter` geometric section headers, top-rule specification ledger cards, and open horizontal-hairline tables.

**Pipeline.** Every chapter is authored in CommonMark Markdown with build-time KaTeX mathematical typesetting and built through the shared multi-volume publishing pipeline (`tools/gen-summary.mjs`, `tools/mdbook-trustchain.mjs`, `tools/diagram_lib.py`, `tools/build-pdf.mjs`, and `tools/audit_book.py`) governed by the craft invariants in `editorial.md`. All forty-three architectural vector diagrams in `books/android/tools/gen_diagrams.py` are programmatically generated and seven-way collision- and orthogonality-verified on every build.

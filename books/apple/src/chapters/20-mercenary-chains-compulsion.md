# Mercenary Zero-Click Chains and Sovereign Cloud Compulsion

## Overview

* **Link in the Chain:** End-to-end architectural synthesis and Finale of Volume II; stress-tests the complete twenty-chapter Apple trust chain from `Boot ROM`, `SEP`, and `SPTM`/`EMTE` through `BlastDoor` and `Advanced Data Protection` (`ADP`) against two apex adversaries: multi-million-dollar mercenary zero-click exploit chains and sovereign statutory compulsion.
* **Core Security Invariant:** Cryptographic primitives (`Image4`, `SEP` `UID`, `PQ3`, `ADP`, `PCC`) never fail mathematically in isolation; vertical trust chains snap exclusively where untrusted bytes encounter Turing-complete parser or IPC logic (*FORCEDENTRY*, *BLASTPASS*, *Coruna*, *DarkSword*), where unmodeled SoC registers bypass CPU monitors (*Operation Triangulation*), or where sovereign statute compels regional feature-enrollment withdrawal (`UK IPA` Section 253).
* **Platform Matrix:** Synthesizes full-chain zero-click and web exploit campaigns targeting `iOS`, `iPadOS`, `macOS`, `watchOS`, and `visionOS` (`CoreGraphics`, `CommCenter`, `FontParser`, `JavaScriptCore`, `ANGLE`, `XNU`, `dyld`) alongside cloud jurisdiction forks (`UK` `ADP` enrollment withdrawal vs. unbackdoored global `Octagon` client code).
* **Trusted Computing Base (TCB):** Every pre-authentication codec, Objective-C `NSXPC` deserialization class, and dynamic linker path reachable prior to `EMTE` fault boundaries; undocumented SoC `MMIO` debug and accelerator blocks; and server-delivered `AuthKit`/`CoreCDP` regional eligibility flags governing `ADP` enrollment.
* **Failure Horizon & Handoff:** Defines the enduring engineering horizon of vertically integrated platforms: custom silicon closes multi-vendor seams and forces attackers into five-to-six-bug chains, yet durable defense requires pairing `A19`/`M5` synchronous `EMTE` and Swift parser rewrites with `Lockdown Mode`, `AFU` inactivity reboots, and absolute refusal to ship client-side key-escrow backdoors under regional legal pressure.

## Threat Model & Design Tension

Step back and survey the unified vertical architecture constructed across Parts I through IV of this volume. At power-on reset, immutable mask `Boot ROM` verifies `TSS`-personalized `Image4` manifests ([Chapter 1](01-secure-boot.md)), confining `macOS` UNIX extensibility inside `1TR` `SEP`-signed `LocalPolicy` ([Chapter 2](02-mac-localpolicy.md)). Isolated `SEP` silicon binds per-file `APFS` Data Protection and Keychain keybags to non-exportable 256-bit `UID` fuses and monotonic anti-replay counters ([Chapter 3](03-secure-enclave.md), [Chapter 10](10-apfs-data-protection.md), [Chapter 11](11-keychain-stolen-device.md)). On the Application Processor (`AP`), `PAC`, `SPTM` (`GL2`), `TXM` (`GL0`), `Exclaves`, and `A19`/`M5` synchronous `EMTE` (*Memory Integrity Enforcement*) strip `EL1` `XNU` of raw page-table authority and trap spatial/temporal heap corruption on first dereference ([Chapter 4](04-silicon-memory-safety.md)). `APFS` `SSV` seals `/` inside a verified Merkle tree ([Chapter 5](05-apfs-ssv-dyld.md)); `Launch Constraints`, `Seatbelt`, and `BlastDoor` cage system daemons and untrusted message parsers ([Chapter 6](06-amfi-txm-notarization.md), [Chapter 7](07-ipc-seatbelt-blastdoor.md)); `TCC` and `EndpointSecurity` govern desktop access and telemetry ([Chapter 8](08-macos-tcc-sysext.md), [Chapter 9](09-esf-xprotect-forensics.md)); and `Hardware Attestation`, `Passkeys`, `DDM`, `Advanced Data Protection` (`ADP`), `iMessage` `PQ3` + `KT`, `Find My`, `Foundation Models` `.onToolCall` guards, and `Private Cloud Compute` extend silicon trust into a zero-knowledge cloud ([Chapter 12](12-sep-app-device-attestation.md) through [Chapter 19](19-private-cloud-compute.md)).

Viewed layer by layer, that twenty-link chain appears almost impervious. Yet between 2021 and 2026, two real-world forces repeatedly penetrated or bypassed its protections without inverting a single `SHA-384` digest, factoring a single key, or solving a single `ML-KEM` lattice instance [19, 21, 23, 27, 526, 527].

First, Tier 3 commercial surveillance vendors (`NSO Group`, `Intellexa`, and the developers behind *Operation Triangulation*, *Coruna*, and *DarkSword*) demonstrated that *tightening every cryptographic and memory-protection seam concentrates offensive investment onto residual stateful complexity* [19, 21, 23, 526, 528]. Whenever Apple sealed off `JIT` compilation, hardened `BlastDoor`, or locked page tables inside `PPL` and `SPTM`, well-capitalized exploit teams responded by synthesizing Turing-complete *weird machines* (`Flake & Bratus` [529]) out of 1990s fax/font decoders, Objective-C `NSExpression` reflection engines, `WebGL` shader translators, and undocumented GPU debug `MMIO` registers exposed on the SoC interconnect [19, 21, 23, 530].

Second, in January and February 2025, Tier 4 sovereign statutory compulsion collided head-on with zero-knowledge cloud storage when the United Kingdom Home Office served Apple with a secret *Technical Capability Notice (`TCN`)* under *Section 253 of the Investigatory Powers Act 2016* (as tightened by the *Investigatory Powers (Amendment) Act 2024* [27, 531]), demanding blanket capability to access encrypted `iCloud` backups worldwide [527, 532]. Analyzing three generations of in-the-wild exploit chains alongside the 2025-2026 UK Investigatory Powers Tribunal (`IPT`) litigation [28, 527, 533] reveals the foundational law governing the entire volume: *silicon fuses and mathematics bound the Trusted Computing Base, whereas parser expressiveness, undocumented hardware state, and jurisdictional enrollment gates define its true perimeter*.

## Silicon & System Mechanics

### Generation 1 (2021): NSO FORCEDENTRY JBIG2 Logic Circuits and NSPredicate Pivots

Captured by Citizen Lab on a Saudi activist's `iPhone` in September 2021 (`Marczak et al.` [250]) and dissected in a landmark two-part analysis by Ian Beer and Samuel Gross at Google Project Zero [21, 530], NSO Group's *FORCEDENTRY* (`CVE-2021-30860`, patched in `iOS 14.8` [534]) demonstrated how attackers defeat both `BlastDoor` ([Chapter 7](07-ipc-seatbelt-blastdoor.md)) and `PAC`/`W^X` ([Chapter 4](04-silicon-memory-safety.md)) without executing a single injected `ARM64` instruction.

![Figure: Evolution of in-the-wild full-chain exploits (FORCEDENTRY 2021, Operation Triangulation 2023, and DarkSword 2025-2026) paired with the exact architectural defenses each campaign forced Apple to build.](diagrams/20-mercenary-zero-click-chains.svg)

First, how did *FORCEDENTRY* reach a rich decoder *before* `BlastDoor` in `iOS 14`? To make animated memes loop seamlessly inside `Messages`, `IMTranscoderAgent` (running *outside* `BlastDoor` prior to `iOS 15.0`) invoked `[IMGIFUtils copyGifFromPath:toDestinationPath:error:]` on any incoming attachment whose filename ended in `.gif` [21]. Under the hood, `IMGIFUtils` handed the file to `ImageIO` / `CoreGraphics`, which ignored the `.gif` extension, sniffed magic bytes, and parsed over twenty image and document formats, including *`CoreGraphics` PDF streams embedding Xpdf `JBIG2` monochrome scanner segments* [21]! Inside `JBIG2Stream::readTextRegionSeg()`, an unsigned 32-bit accumulator `numSyms += ((JBIG2SymbolDict *)seg)->getSize()` suffered a classic integer wraparound (`CVE-2021-30860`), allocating an undersized `syms` heap buffer and overflowing `JBIG2Bitmap` pointers across the adjacent `GList` segment vector and current page `JBIG2Bitmap` struct (`w`, `h`, and `line` stride fields) [21]. Because `JBIG2Bitmap` inherits from `JBIG2Segment`, `seg->getType()` passed `PAC` virtual-call checks cleanly while halting the overflow at an exact byte offset, leaving the page bitmap's width and height unbounded across the entire user-space heap [21]!

Inside a `JIT`-less process where `PAC` signed code pointers and `W^X` blocked shellcode, NSO turned `JBIG2`'s *refinement coding* operator (which combines reference and canvas bitmaps via bitwise `AND`, `OR`, `XOR`, and `XNOR`) into a *virtual boolean CPU* [21, 529]. Defining over *70,000 `JBIG2` segment commands*, the PDF wired 1-bit pixel operations into 32-bit ripple-carry adders, bit-shifters, comparators, and conditional pointer dereferences directly inside heap memory! That boolean computer scanned the heap to bypass `ASLR`, forged an Objective-C object on pre-`14.5`/untagged `isa` layouts, and triggered `dealloc` solely to deserialize an embedded `bplist00` `NSKeyedArchiver` graph of `NSFunctionExpression` objects [530].

Examine why Stage 2 (*FORCEDENTRY*'s `IMTranscoderAgent` sandbox escape, `Beer & Gross` [530]) remains a sobering warning even in the `EMTE` era: *it used zero memory corruption whatsoever*! Since OS X 10.5, `Foundation`'s `NSPredicate` / `NSExpression` query language supported `FUNCTION(...)` and `CAST(..., 'Class')` reflection operators capable of invoking arbitrary Objective-C selectors [530]. After deleting its own `.gif` delivery trace from `/tmp/com.apple.messages`, the first `NSFunctionExpression` connected to `CommCenter` over `com.apple.commcenter.xpc` (`[CoreTelephonyClient context:evaluateMobileSubscriberIdentity:]`), whose `NSXPC` protocol foolishly typed its second parameter as generic `(NSObject *)arg2` (admitting *any* `NSSecureCoding` subclass in the `dyld` shared cache!) [530]. Although `NSPredicate` normally disables evaluation upon `initWithCoder:` until `allowEvaluation` is called, NSO passed a two-element `NSArray`: element `0` (`AVSpeechSynthesisVoice`) abused Objective-C `+initialize` side effects to force `CommCenter` to dynamically load `AXSpeechImplementation.bundle` and its transitive dependency `PrototypeTools.framework`; element `1` deserialized a `PTSection` containing a `PTRow` whose `[PTRow initWithCoder:]` blindly called `[self->condition allowEvaluation]` before `[PTSection _shouldEnableRow:]` evaluated the attacker's `NSPredicate` inside `CommCenter` [530]! Six nested `NSFunctionExpression` calls (using `[CaliCalendarAnonymizer sharedAnonymizedStrings]` as a global variable dictionary) fingerprinted `[AKDevice _hardwareModel]` and `_buildNumber`, fetched an `AES-128`-encrypted next-stage plist over HTTPS, and pivoted to kernel exploitation, prompting Apple to banish `GIF` transcoding into `BlastDoor` (`iOS 15.0`), strip `CAST` and object-creation side effects from `NSExpression` (`iOS 15.1`), and gate `PrototypeTools` predicate decoding behind `os_variant_allows_internal_security_policies()` [21, 530, 534].

### Generation 2 (2023): Operation Triangulation ADJUST Overflow and Silicon MMIO DMA

Two years later, Kaspersky GReAT researchers (`Larin, Kucherin, Bezvershenko, Kuznetsov, and Pashkov` [19, 535, 536]) uncovered *Operation Triangulation* targeting `iOS` up to `16.2` via a four-zero-day zero-click `iMessage` chain (`CVE-2023-41990`, `CVE-2023-32434`, `CVE-2023-38606`, and `CVE-2023-32435`, patched in `iOS 16.5.1` and `16.6` [537]). By 2023, `PAC` protected Objective-C `isa` pointers and hardware `PPL` (`APRR` page-table locks from [Chapter 4](04-silicon-memory-safety.md)) blocked `EL1` kernel R/W exploits from modifying page tables on `A12` through `A16` SoCs.

![Figure: Operation Triangulation (2023) four-zero-day exploit architecture tracing CVE-2023-41990 TrueType ADJUST overflow, JavaScriptCore DollarVM PAC bypass, CVE-2023-32434 XNU VM mapping, and CVE-2023-38606 undocumented gfx-asc MMIO DMA around PPL.](diagrams/20-extra-triangulation-four-stage-bypass.svg)

Follow each link of Figure 2's vertical bypass [19, 535, 536]:

At Stage 1, an invisible `iMessage` attachment delivered a crafted TrueType font exploiting `CVE-2023-41990`, an out-of-bounds write inside an undocumented, Apple-proprietary `ADJUST` TrueType bytecode instruction dating back to early-1990s Macintosh font hinting [19, 536, 537]. Using ROP/JOP and `NSExpression` stages without `JIT`, the payload patched `JavaScriptCore` runtime flags in memory to enable `JSC`'s internal `DollarVM` (`$vm`) debugging facility, executing an 11,000-line obfuscated JavaScript stage equipped with a `PAC` signing bypass [19, 536].

At Stage 2, that user-space script triggered `CVE-2023-32434`, an integer overflow inside `XNU`'s `mach_make_memory_entry` and `vm_map` Mach virtual-memory syscalls, granting the unprivileged user process direct read/write mappings over *the entire physical DRAM of the iPhone* [19, 536]! Even holding physical RAM mappings from user space, however, CPU core `APRR`/`PPL` permissions still faulted any direct CPU store targeting `PPL`-protected kernel page tables [19].

Stage 3 (`CVE-2023-38606`, dubbed *"The Last Hardware Mystery"* at 37C3 [19, 536]) bypassed `PPL` without touching `PPL` code at all: *it routed around the CPU core through undocumented Apple Silicon hardware `MMIO` registers*! First, the exploit checked `cpuid` (`A12` `0x07D34B9F` through `A16` `0x8765EDEA`) and wrote power-enable commands (`0x1F0023FF`) to the `GFX` power-manager `MMIO` register (`0x23B080388`..`0x23B700408`) [19]. Next, it accessed `0x206040000`, an unadvertised proprietary Apple `UTT` CoreSight debug register block belonging to the `gfx-asc` GPU coprocessor (`ml_dbgwrap_halt_cpu`), halting the GPU coprocessor core cleanly before touching unmapped addresses (`0x206140008`, `0x206140108`, `0x206150020`, `0x206150040`, and `0x206150048`) that *did not appear in any `DeviceTree`, `iBoot`, kernelcache, or GPU firmware binary* [19]! Writing a target physical address, data word, and hardware hash into those undocumented `gfx-asc` registers instructed the SoC fabric itself to DMA-write directly into `PPL`-protected physical memory outside the CPU MMU's `PPL` checks [19]! After gaining arbitrary `PPL`-bypassing control, the JS stage injected `IMAgent` to scrub forensic database traces, spawned an invisible `Safari` view (`CVE-2023-32435`) to fingerprint the target and re-exploit `CVE-2023-32434` + `CVE-2023-38606` from a Mach-O binary, and deployed the in-memory `TriangleDB` implant [19, 535]. Apple responded by fencing those `MMIO` ranges in boot firmware (`iOS 16.5.1`/`16.6` [537]), migrating `A17 Pro+` page-table and `IOMMU` ownership into `SPTM` (`GL2`), and completely replacing decades-old C TrueType font rendering code with memory-safe Swift!

### Generation 3 (2023-2026): BLASTPASS, Coruna, and DarkSword vs. SPTM and EMTE

As `BlastDoor` expanded, `Lockdown Mode` disabled high-risk codecs, and `SPTM`/`TXM` sealed `GL2`/`GL0` page tables, mercenary campaigns adapted along two telling trajectories between late 2023 and 2026 [2, 23, 65, 526, 528]. First, in September 2023, Citizen Lab captured NSO Group's zero-click *BLASTPASS* chain (`CVE-2023-41064` buffer overflow in `ImageIO` lossless `WebP` canonical Huffman table construction paired with `CVE-2023-41061` in `PassKit` Apple Wallet `.pkpass` attachments, patched in `iOS 16.6.1` [65, 538]), while empirically confirming that *`Lockdown Mode` blocked live `BLASTPASS` attacks completely in the wild*!

Second, with zero-click `iMessage` surfaces heavily narrowed, surveillance vendors (`Predator` [528]) and multi-actor exploit brokers shifted toward multi-hop browser chains, culminating in the March 2026 disclosures of *Coruna* (`GTIG` and `iVerify` [526]) and *DarkSword* (`GTIG`, `iVerify`, and `Lookout` [23, 539]). *Coruna* exposed a sobering supply-chain proliferation lifecycle: a single commercial framework housing *five full `iOS` exploit chains and 23 distinct exploits* migrated within twelve months from a commercial spyware customer (`Feb 2025`) to state espionage (`UNC6353` against Ukrainian targets) and finally mass financial/cryptocurrency theft waterholes [526]! Immediately thereafter, *DarkSword* (`iOS 18.4`-`18.7`, `< 26.3`) required chaining *six separate vulnerabilities* (`CVE-2025-31277` and `CVE-2025-43529` in `WebKit`/`JSC`, `CVE-2025-14174` in `ANGLE` `WebGL` escaping `WebContent` into the `GPU` process, `CVE-2025-43510` `XNU` virtual-memory copy-on-write pivoting into `mediaplaybackd`, `CVE-2025-43520`, and `CVE-2026-20700` `dyld` state corruption [23, 539]) to deploy the `GHOSTBLADE` infostealer!

Stepping back to compare these campaigns side by side reveals how every silicon and OS hardening boundary forced adversaries onto narrower, costlier ground [2, 19, 21, 23, 65, 526]:

### Sovereign Compulsion Against Zero-Knowledge Cloud: UK IPA Section 253

Now shift from technical zero-days to *Tier 4 sovereign legal compulsion* [27, 28, 527, 531]. Under *Section 253 of the UK Investigatory Powers Act 2016 (`IPA`)*, the Home Secretary may serve a telecommunications or cloud operator with a *Technical Capability Notice (`TCN`)* requiring maintenance of the capability to remove electronic protection applied by or on behalf of that operator, backed by criminal non-disclosure penalties [27]. Effective October 14, 2024, the *Investigatory Powers (Amendment) Act 2024 (`c. 9`)* tightened that regime further, expanding extraterritorial reach and empowering the Home Office to freeze security upgrades pending review [531].

![Figure: The 2025-2026 UK Investigatory Powers Act Section 253 dispute illustrating why Apple withdrew new Advanced Data Protection (ADP) enrollment in the UK rather than introducing a global client-side escrow backdoor.](diagrams/20-uk-ipa-s253-adp-boundary.svg)

In January 2025 (first reported by *The Washington Post* on February 7, 2025 [532] and confirmed in subsequent tribunal judgments [527]), the UK Home Office served Apple with an unprecedented Section 253 `TCN` demanding blanket technical capability to access fully encrypted `iCloud` data worldwide, without territorial limitation to British subjects [527, 532].

Examine the immovable cryptographic wall separating *Standard Data Protection* from *Advanced Data Protection (`ADP`)* ([Chapter 15](15-icloud-hsm-adp.md)) under such a notice [28, 540, 541]:

Under `ADP`, Apple's cloud servers hold *zero* `CloudKit` service keys ($K_{\text{service}}$) for `iCloud Backup`, `Photos`, `Drive`, or `Notes`; those keys originate inside the user's `SEP` and synchronize exclusively across the user's `Octagon` trusted-device circle. Consequently, no server-side configuration change in a UK or US data center can decrypt an `ADP` user's vault! Compliance via "exceptional access" would require Apple to *author, sign, and push a subverted `iOS` and `macOS` OS update (`secd` / `Octagon`) that covertly wraps user `ADP` circle keys to an authority-escrowed public key $PK_{\text{escrow}}$ whenever a server policy flag requests it*!

As fifteen of the world's foremost cryptographers proved in *Keys Under Doormats* (`Abelson et al.` [540]), *a jurisdiction-scoped client-side key-escrow backdoor does not exist in mathematics*:

$$\text{EscrowHook}\bigl(\texttt{Octagon}_{\text{client}},\, PK_{\text{escrow}}\bigr) \implies \text{Global Single Point of Catastrophic Failure}$$

Once `Octagon` client code on 1.5 billion devices contains a latent escrow branch triggered by `AuthKit` or `IDS`/`CloudKit` flags, any foreign intelligence agency, supply-chain attacker, or rogue insider compromising that flag server or stealing $SK_{\text{escrow}}$ unlocks encrypted vaults in Washington, Berlin, Tokyo, and London alike [540, 541] (while directly implicating US constitutional/statutory bars under the `CLOUD Act`, `18 U.S.C. 2713`, which explicitly disclaims mandatory decryption [542], and European human-rights proportionality jurisprudence established by the ECtHR in *Podchasov v. Russia*, `2024` [543]).

Because `ADP` enrollment is gated per `Apple Account` storefront by `AuthKit`/`CoreCDP` *before* keys are generated, Apple executed the only response that obeyed UK domestic law without poisoning global `Octagon` client code [28]: on *February 21, 2025*, Apple withdrew `ADP` enrollment for new UK accounts and began phasing out existing UK `ADP` enrollments (reverting UK accounts to *Standard Data Protection*, while `iCloud Keychain`, `Passwords`, `Health`, `iMessage` `PQ3`, and `FaceTime` remained E2EE!) while reiterating publicly that Apple has never built a backdoor or master key into its products and never will [28]. Simultaneously, Apple challenged the `TCN` before the UK *Investigatory Powers Tribunal (`IPT`)*. When the Home Office demanded that the March 14, 2025 hearing take place in *total secrecy* without even listing the case or party names, Lord Justice Singh, Mr Justice Johnson, and Boyd KC issued a landmark open-justice judgment on *April 7, 2025* (*Apple Inc. v. Secretary of State for the Home Department*, `[2025] UKIPT 2` [527]), rejecting the secrecy bid as contrary to open justice alongside parallel civil-liberties challenges by `Privacy International` and `Liberty` [544]. Following August 2025 US/UK diplomatic engagement in which the UK dropped extraterritorial claims over US citizens and issued a narrowed UK-resident `TCN` in autumn 2025 [545], Apple filed a *renewed `IPT` legal challenge in July-August 2026* contesting the statutory authority and proportionality of the revised notice itself [533]. Notice why even a territorially narrowed UK-resident `TCN` leaves the underlying architectural conflict unresolved [533, 540]: `Apple Account` country codes change when users relocate across borders, multinational families share E2EE `iCloud` Shared Photo Libraries and `CloudKit` zones across jurisdictions, and forcing British citizens onto server-escrowed *Standard Data Protection* strips domestic hospitals, journalists, parliamentarians, and infrastructure engineers of their strongest cryptographic defense against foreign ransomware and espionage [541, 544].

## Platform Forks Across Apple OSes

Across all twenty chapters, our five Apple Silicon environments exhibit distinct resilience profiles against these apex threats [2, 21, 23, 28]. On `iOS`, `iPadOS`, and `watchOS`, enabling *Lockdown Mode* ([Chapter 10](10-apfs-data-protection.md)) deterministically disables `JSC` `JIT` (`CVE-2025-43529`), legacy `JBIG2`/font/WebP pre-rendering (*FORCEDENTRY*, *Triangulation*, *BLASTPASS*), and locked USB data paths (`CVE-2025-24200`) in one switch [23, 65]. `visionOS` inherits `iOS`'s `SPTM`/`Launch Constraints` spine while isolating raw cameras inside the `R1` co-processor ([Chapter 13](13-accounts-passkeys-continuity.md)). `macOS` gives defenders unmatched visibility (`EndpointSecurity` [Chapter 9](09-esf-xprotect-forensics.md)) and `Virtualization.framework` micro-VMs ([Chapter 18](18-agentic-ai-workload-isolation.md)), while demanding strict `Full Security` `LocalPolicy`, `SDP`/`Keychain` hygiene, and `TCC` separation. Finally, `Private Cloud Compute` ([Chapter 19](19-private-cloud-compute.md)) and `iMessage` `KT` ([Chapter 16](16-imessage-pq3-kt.md)) demonstrate why *public cryptographic Transparency Logs* represent the ultimate defense against secret compulsion: unlike `AuthKit` enrollment flags (which can be toggled regionally in the UK [28]), a backdoor in `PCC` or `KT` cannot be deployed secretly without publishing immutable cryptographic proof to the world.

> **Key idea.**
> *The Apple Trust Chain* closes on a single unifying principle: *cryptographic keys and silicon monitors shrink the Trusted Computing Base to verifiable hardware and audited code, while parser complexity, shared peripherals, ambient host delegation, and statutory jurisdiction define where real-world compromises occur.* Mastering platform security means engineering and verifying *both* halves simultaneously: never mistaking a valid signature for safe parser input, an unlocked `AFU` session for `BFU` storage, a terminal's `TCC` grant for safe agent isolation, or Standard cloud storage for `Advanced Data Protection`.
